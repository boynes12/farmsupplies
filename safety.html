Responsible crop protection

Healthy crops. Safer handling.

Crop-protection products should always be used according to their manufacturer label and applicable local rules. Here's how we help every customer choose and handle them responsibly.

Before you spray

Choosing the right product

Not every crop-protection product suits every crop, pest, or growth stage. Tell us the crop, the pest, weed, or disease you're managing, and your target timing — we'll help you match it to a labelled, appropriate product rather than guessing.

Core guidelines

Follow these every time.

⌁

Step 1

Read the label

Always read and follow the product label before use — it's the legal and safety reference for rate, timing, and precautions.

⌁

Step 2

Wear PPE

Use the personal protective equipment recommended on the label — gloves, eye protection, and appropriate clothing at minimum.

◒

Step 3

Store securely

Keep all crop-protection products in original, labelled containers, away from children, food, water sources, and animals.

✤

Step 4

Dispose responsibly

Rinse and dispose of empty containers according to local regulations — never reuse them for food, water, or feed.

In an emergency

If something goes wrong

If a product is ingested, splashed in the eyes, or causes a skin reaction, follow the first-aid instructions on the product label immediately and contact your local poison control center or emergency services. Bring the product label or container with you if you seek medical care.

If you're ever unsure whether a symptom or exposure needs urgent attention, treat it as an emergency and seek help right away.
